About Clyra

Painted by hand, made for every kitchen.

Clyra Ceramics makes hand-painted storage jars, oil and vinegar bottles, salad and handi sets, and dinnerware. Our designs draw on Mughal art, Jaipuri painting and Indian heritage patterns, finished with turned wooden lids.

We sell to homes and supply retailers, cafés, hotels and corporate gifting teams in India and worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the pieces you like to your enquiry, fill in the contact form and press Send. It reaches us by email, or you can message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm prices, stock and delivery before you pay.

Do you take bulk and wholesale orders?

Yes. We supply retailers, cafés, restaurants, hotels and companies. Choose "Business" in the form and tell us the quantities you need.

Can I get a custom colour or design?

For larger orders we can discuss colour changes and custom designs. Describe what you have in mind in your enquiry.

Do you deliver outside India?

Yes, we deliver worldwide. Every piece is packed carefully for transit. Shipping cost and delivery time depend on the destination and order size.

Are the pieces food safe?

Our jars and serveware are made for everyday kitchen use. Ask us about care for any specific piece.

Why does my piece look slightly different from the photo?

Each piece is painted by hand, so brushwork and colour vary slightly. No two are exactly alike.
